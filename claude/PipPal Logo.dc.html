2Rebuilt — one gesture each, on a 120-unit construction grid

Turn 1 was weak and you were right to say so. The marks were axis-aligned rectangles assembled on a grid: no stroke system, no counterform, no optical correction, and nothing that resolved into a single shape. These three are built differently.

One gesture

Each mark is a single move, not two ideas stacked. If you can describe it in one clause, it survives; if it needs "and", it doesn't.

One mass

120-unit artboard over a 15-unit reference grid. In the monogram marks the load-bearing rule is a single 20-unit mass for stem and bowl alike, so nothing in them disappears first when it shrinks. The two pictorial marks in this turn are built from contrasting weights instead — 2a runs a 12-unit wick against a 16-unit arm and a 30-unit body, 2c a 10-unit wick against a 72-unit page — and each card names what that costs.

Optical correction

The grid gets it 99% there. Curves overshoot flats, and mass is nudged against its mathematical centre. Each card names the correction it carries.

Icon ≠ logo

iOS 26 rounds and glazes a full-bleed opaque square you don't control, so the icon is drawn as its own bold silhouette — no thin lines, no text, centred inside the mask.
