02 · What the research adds

01
Friction is the whole product risk

Every source names the same failure: traders abandon journals within weeks because manual entry is tedious, and at 5+ trades a day they start skipping fields. PipPal is manual by design — no broker sync — so the two-step sheet is the single most important screen in the app, not the dashboard.

TradeZella guide, 2026 · tradetanto.com · smctradeonline.com
02
Three numbers, not nine

Practitioner guidance converges on win rate, average R and profit factor as the only metrics worth surfacing first. The spec's Analytics screen asks for nine cards in a horizontal scroller — worth questioning, since a dashboard nobody can read in five seconds gets abandoned too.

edgeflo.com · gaspntrader.com
03
Statistics need ~30–50 trades

Basic patterns need 30–50 trades; confident decisions need 100+. The spec unlocks Analytics at 5 trades. The design should show provisional numbers honestly rather than presenting a 5-trade win rate as fact.

gaspntrader.com, 2026
04
Pip maths is not one formula

Pip size is 0.0001 for most pairs, 0.01 for JPY pairs and gold, 0.001 for silver — and pip value depends on lot size and account currency. The sheet's live "+48 pips | $96.00" readout needs the instrument to drive decimal places, and needs a graceful state when the pair is unknown.

forexpipcalculator.com · myfxbook · infinityalgo
05
The calendar is the hook

Across competitors the green/red month grid is the most-cited feature, and the most-cited gap is a day cell that does nothing when tapped. The spec's day-detail drawer is the right call and should be built early, not last.

TraderSync reviews · TradesViz · Tradevia

03 · The one conflict I cannot resolve alone

Spec tokens

Inter, 12px radii, warm cream, soft borders. Client-selected, documented down to hex, and what the Flutter developer will build against.

Modernist (attached to this project)

Archivo, zero radius, 2px rules, near-mono red on light ground. Sharper and more editorial — this brief is set in it.

They want opposite things: one softens, the other sharpens. My recommendation is to build PipPal on the spec's tokens — they are the client's brand and the developer's contract — and borrow only Modernist's discipline: flush-left labels, honest rules instead of decorative shadows, and restraint with the accent. But this is your call, and it is the first question in the form.

STEP 1

Tokens, type scale, buttons, inputs, cards, tags, nav — as a live component sheet.

STEP 2
The core four

Dashboard, Journal, Calendar, Analytics in a working iPhone frame with real tab switching.

STEP 3
Log Trade + Detail

The two-step sheet with live pip maths, then Trade Detail and its edit mode.

STEP 4
Edges

Auth and onboarding, empty and loading states, settings, dark mode.
